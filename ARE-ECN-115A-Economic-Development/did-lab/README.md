# DiD Lab

Interactive difference-in-differences lesson designed for **ARE/ECN 115A — Economic Development**, an undergraduate course at the University of California, Davis.

The guided case uses Card and Krueger’s 1994 minimum-wage study to introduce the counterfactual, parallel trends, and the two-period DiD estimator.
