# RCT Lab

Interactive randomized controlled trial lesson designed for **ARE/ECN 115A — Economic Development**, an undergraduate course at the University of California, Davis.

The guided example uses Miguel and Kremer’s 2004 school-based deworming study to introduce potential outcomes, the counterfactual, selection bias, and random assignment.
